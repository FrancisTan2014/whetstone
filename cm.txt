feat(editor): add contextual block gutter commands, menu, and wash (#590)

Integrate Tiptap's official React drag-handle to reveal a contextual block
gutter with a block-actions menu, plus keyboard (Shift+F10) and touch-first
(compact "More block actions" trigger) access, and a transient block wash.

- blockGutterCommands: pure block command/query module (turn into, insert
  above/below, duplicate, move up/down, delete) that preserves node ids.
- BlockActionsMenu: Radix dropdown wiring the commands with disabled states.
- blockGutterHighlight: ProseMirror plugin painting a transient wash on the
  active block.
- BlockGutterHandle: pointer-only official DragHandle + grip menu (browser-only
  seam, coverage-excluded like other detached-portal/browser code).
- RichContentEditor: integrate the gutter, Shift+F10 handler, wash lifecycle,
  and the always-rendered compact More trigger.

Deps: the official @tiptap/extension-drag-handle-react stack. drag-handle
declares y-tiptap, extension-collaboration, and extension-node-range as
peerDependencies, so they are installed to satisfy the peer graph only --
collaboration/Yjs is NOT enabled (no Collaboration extension or Y.Doc is
constructed).

100% coverage on all new logic modules; the browser-only DragHandle seam is
narrowly excluded with justification.
